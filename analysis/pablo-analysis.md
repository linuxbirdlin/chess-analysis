## Tactical Patterns

### Strengths
1. **Defensive resilience in Kan structures**: Pablo's Sicilian Kan is well-grounded. He understands the queenside counterplay (...b5, ...Rc8) and is patient in equal positions.
2. **Endgame tenacity**: Multiple games go 80+ moves showing fight (Blitz R5 178 moves, Blitz R7 181 moves). He doesn't resign easily.
3. **Tactical awareness in complications**: Beat Fogo Esquivel 2211 in a 83-move Ruy Lopez endgame conversion; won vs Tran 2023 in English Attack where he maintained pressure.
4. **Patience as Black**: Copa R5 — drew FM 2241 using QGD Capablanca, controlled the position.

### Weaknesses
1. **Early tactical collapses**: vs Zacarias 2219 (Revolucion R1) — lost in 13 moves only. vs various opponents he shows early errors.
2. **As White with Ruy Lopez**: Record 1W–5L+ as White vs 1...e5. Suggests his Ruy Lopez preparation is surface-level.
3. **Under time pressure in endgames**: Copa R4 — blundered a drawn position into a loss in the endgame (multiple blunders vs Leal Escamilla 1990 after having a winning advantage in the middlegame — gave up a full rook).
4. **Against very strong opposition**: Consistently loses to 2100+ opponents. His results against sub-1900 are much better.
5. **Blitz/rapid form**: Weaker results in shorter time controls, consistent with an improvement phase player.

Pablo plays **1...c5 2...e6 4...a6** (Kan/Taimanov) as his primary defense. Austin's normal Rossolimo (3.Bb5) specifically requires 2...Nc6. Against Pablo's 2...e6 setup, Austin must prepare:
- **English Attack**: 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 e6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 a6 5.Nc3 d6 6.Be3 Nf6 7.f3 b5 8.g4 (B41/B43)
- **Alapin**: 1.e4 c5 2.c3 → Pablo must respond with ...Nf6 or ...d5; positions favorable to Austin

Pablo has no documented games against the English Attack 8.g4! setup — this would be new territory for him while Austin has practiced it (vs Zachary, Pranav, Emerson).

### Austin as Black vs Pablo's 1.e4
Pablo uses English Attack or Alapin vs Sicilian. Austin plays Sicilian — should use the **Taimanov/Kan** (same structure Pablo uses as Black). This creates mirror territory where Austin, having prepared this structure vs multiple opponents, may be more prepared than Pablo is as White with it.
