## 3. Key Strengths & Areas for Improvement

### What Went Well (Strengths)
1. **Strategic Intent & Space Control:** You naturally build strong pawn centers, take control of open files, and press on the correct side of the board in positional setups (R1, R2, R4).
2. **Endgame Resourcefulness:** In complex endings (such as Round 1), you demonstrate accurate calculation and tactical awareness under defensive pressure.

---

### Recurring Common Mistakes & Actionable Advice

#### 1. Speculative & Unsound Sacrifices
* **Pattern:** Played $10.\text{Bxh7+}$ (R3) without fully calculating $11...\text{Kg6!}$, and $5.\text{b4}$ (R6) without sufficient compensation.
* **Action Item:** Apply the **"Escape Square Check"** rule: Before playing a sacrifice, explicitly verify if the opponent's king has non-linear escape squares (like g6 or f7) that prevent a forced line or continuous tempos.

#### 2. Tactical Blind Spots in Winning Positions
* **Pattern:** Missing dynamic tactic $20.\text{Ne4!}$ in Round 4 while mounting a kingside assault.
* **Action Item:** When building an attack, dedicate 1–2 minutes specifically to looking for **unconventional checks, sacrifices, and overloaded defenders** before opting for passive/positional moves.

#### 3. King Exposure in Open Center Positions
* **Pattern:** Delayed castling led to severe penalties in Round 3 and Round 6 when central lines opened up.
* **Action Item:** Prioritize early castling in gambit lines or open games before launching aggressive flank pawn moves.
