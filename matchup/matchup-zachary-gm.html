The Training Rule

Before playing Qh5 in any attacking position — ask: "Can I play Bxh7+ first?"
The Bxh7+/Qh5+/Rd3-h3 pattern is a complete attacking system. The queen reaching h5 alone achieves nothing if Black can play ...g6. The bishop must go first.
