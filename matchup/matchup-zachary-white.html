§5 — Model Game / Reference Plan

The English Attack (Be3 / f3 / Qd2 / O-O-O / g4) against the Taimanov was Kasparov and Anand's main weapon in the 1990s B47 battles. The structural plan:

  1. Development (moves 6–8): Be3, f3, set up Qd2. Do not castle kingside — always O-O-O.
  2. Storm launch (moves 9–12): Qd2, O-O-O, g5. Rg1 supports the g-pawn advance.
  3. Kingside attack (moves 13–18): g5-g6 pawn wedge, h4-h5 supporting the attack, Ne4-Nd6 knight activation.
  4. Black counterplay handling: If Black plays ...b4 (queenside attack on Nc3), respond with Nd5! — the knight leap is stronger than retreating. If Black plays ...f5, respond with e5! central counter-blow.
Key lesson from the direct encounter + personal study: At move 14, Austin had +3.79 and missed 14.Bxh7+! Kxh7 15.Qh5+ Kg8 16.g5 — a standard bishop-sacrifice attacking theme. The English Attack creates these same sacrifice opportunities at moves 12–18.
