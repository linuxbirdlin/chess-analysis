## Austin as White

The Sveshnikov is one of the most tactical and double-edged Sicilian systems. Austin needs to be prepared.

**Austin's standard Sicilian response:** Rossolimo (3.Bb5) — this **sidesteps the Sveshnikov entirely** since the Sveshnikov requires 3.d4, and Rossolimo plays 3.Bb5 instead.

### Recommended Weapon

**Rossolimo Sicilian** — 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5

- Avoids all Sveshnikov mainline theory (Yaen's prepared ground)
- Austin has shown Rossolimo preparation in other matchups
- After 3...g6 4.Bxc6 dxc6 5.d3 or 4.O-O (fianchetto setup): white gets structural advantage, Yaen must solve the doubled pawns
- After 3...e6: enters Rossolimo mainlines

**Key variation to know:** 3.Bb5 Nc6 → possible 4.Bxc6 (exchange variation) or 4.O-O / 4.c3 (holding the bishop).

**Alternative:** Open Sicilian 3.d4 but prepare Anti-Sveshnikov (4.Nxd4 e5 5.Nb5 d6 6.c4 Nf6 7.N1c3) — requires deep preparation.

### Key Themes
1. **Rossolimo cuts off Sveshnikov prep** — Yaen's deep preparation is in B32/B33; Rossolimo gives unfamiliar territory
2. **Exploit doubled c-pawns** — Rossolimo exchange (Bxc6) gives Black structural problems
