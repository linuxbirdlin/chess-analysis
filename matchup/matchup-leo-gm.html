Part 2 — Austin as Black: Queen's Indian vs Catalan

Core fact: Leo plays 3.Nf3 specifically to avoid the Nimzo-Indian (3.Nc3 Bb4). Austin's standard weapon (Nimzo) is not available. The correct antidote: Queen's Indian (3...b6) or Bogo-Indian (3...Bb4+) — both force Leo out of his ideal Catalan setup and give Austin active piece play.

Austin's weapon: Queen's Indian

Leo: 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6 3.Nf3 b6! 4.g3 Bb7 5.Bg2 Be7 6.O-O O-O 7...Ne4! — Ne4 is Austin's signature QI move: centralizes the knight, pressures c3, and forces Leo to react. Leo cannot continue his ideal Catalan setup without conceding something.
Austin: 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6 3.Nf3 b6! 4.g3 Bb7 5.Bg2 Be7 6.O-O O-O 7.Nc3! Ne4! 8.Qc2 Nxc3 9.Qxc3 d5! 10.cxd5 exd5 — After ...Ne4 Nxc3 ...d5, Austin has a solid IQP position. The Bb7 fires down the long diagonal; d5 pawn is a strong outpost. Leo's Bg2 now faces Austin's Bb7 in a strategic battle Austin understands.

Plan: After 10…exd5, Austin has an IQP position — Austin's strength (same as Nimzo-Indian). The Bb7 pressures the long diagonal; ...Ba6 is always possible to attack c4 if needed (same as Austin's Nimzo habit). Leo's Catalan setup is broken.

Austin: 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6 3.Nf3 Bb4+! 4.Bd2 Bxd2+ 5.Qxd2 O-O 6.g3 d5! 7.Bg2 c6 8...b6! — Bogo-Indian: after Bxd2+ Qxd2, Leo has the bishop pair but Austin has a solid position. d5+c6 structure is very solid; ...b6 prepares ...Ba6 to attack c4 (Austin's Nimzo habit applies).

The Bogo-Indian gives Austin an immediate piece imbalance (Leo's bishop pair vs Austin's active rook placement). Not as sharp as Queen's Indian but solid. Leo's Catalan g2 bishop stares at nothing useful after Black plays ...d5/...c6.

Austin: 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6 3.Nf3 d5 4.g3 dxc4! 5.Bg2 Nc6! 6.O-O Rb8 7.e3 b5! 8.a4 b4 — Open Catalan: grab the c4 pawn immediately! ...Nc6 + ...Rb8 + ...b5 defends the pawn and creates queenside counterplay. Leo's Bg2 is temporarily pointing at nothing.

The Open Catalan (grabbing c4) is a concrete imbalance — Leo must spend moves recovering the pawn. Austin's ...b5/...b4 creates immediate queenside play. This is the most tactical option vs Leo's Catalan.
