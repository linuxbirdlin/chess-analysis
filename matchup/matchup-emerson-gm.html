Key choice: After 3.Bb5+ Bd7 4.Bxd7+, Austin should play 4...Nxd7! (NOT 4...Qxd7). The knight recapture keeps the queen on the board, maintains piece complexity, and avoids Emerson's prepared technical endgame structure.
