Part 1 — Austin as White: Bc4/Bb3 Anti-Scandinavian + Exchange Caro-Kann

Main Line — Scandinavian Modern 3…Nf6 (Bc4/Bb3 system)

White has dominant central control and a clear queenside-to-kingside plan. The Nb6 is passive, and Black's fianchetto doesn't provide the usual Scandinavian counterplay. Austin's IQP and central-pressure experience from Exchange CK structures applies here. Target: d5 pawn advance to open the center after ...e6 is provoked.
