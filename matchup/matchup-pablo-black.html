§2 — Recommended Defense: Sicilian Taimanov (Mirror Play)

Key insight: Pablo plays the Sicilian Kan/Taimanov as Black — it's his best-prepared defense. Austin should play the SAME structure as Black vs Pablo's 1.e4. This creates mirror territory where Austin has deeply studied the structure from the White side (vs Pranav, Zachary, Emerson, Aarush) and can exploit it from the Black side.

Main Line: Taimanov B48 vs English Attack

Pablo: 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 e6 5.Nc3 a6 6.Be3 Nge7 7.f3 d6 8.Qd2 Nxd4! — CRITICAL defensive move. Trading the Nd4 BEFORE Pablo plays 9.g4 defuses the English Attack completely. After 9.Bxd4 Nc6, Black has a solid Taimanov position with no kingside attack coming. Austin has drilled this exact technique vs multiple opponents.
MAIN After 8…Nxd4! — Solid Taimanov
8.Qd2 Nxd4! 9.Bxd4 Nc6 10.Be3 Be7 11.O-O-O O-O → Solid position, no kingside attack, Austin has queenside counterplay

After 9.Bxd4 Nc6 10.Be3 Be7, White has no g4 attack (no knight on d4 to support it). Austin's position is solid with …b5, …Bb7, …d5 counterplay options. Pablo is out of his prepared English Attack lines.

What NOT to do: If Austin delays …Nxd4 and Pablo plays 9.g4!, the English Attack is on and Black is in danger. The trade on move 8 is critical — don't wait until g4 is already played.

Alternative move order: Pure Kan (4…a6)

Pablo: 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 e6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 a6 5.Nc3 Qc7 (or 5…Nf6) 6.Be3 Nf6 7.f3 d6 8.Qd2 Nbd7 — If Austin plays 2…e6 (pure Kan move order), the ideas are the same. Trade the Nd4 with …Nxd4 when the opportunity arises. After trading, Black has solid Kan structure without White's attack.
