Critical Prophylaxis Rules

Rule 1 — Always play h3 before Bg5. In Copa R1, Queretaro R1, and Copa R5, Inti played ...Bg4 to pin the f3-knight. The pin disrupts White's coordination. h3 on move 7–8 is mandatory.
Rule 2 — Watch for ...Nh5-Nf4. Inti's standard tactical maneuver is ...Nh5 (attacking Bg5 or going to f4). In Copa R1 (move 13), Queretaro R1 (move 10), Copa R5 (move 9), and Agua R1 (move 13) — he played ...Nh5 in every Italian game. Calculate ...Nf4 consequences before releasing central tension. Answer with g3 if needed.
Rule 3 — Don't rush d4. Build Re1, h3, Nbd2, Nf1-Ng3 first. d4 should arrive when Black's pieces are already committed. Premature d4 hands Black the d5 outpost and equalizes.
