Part 3 — Key Tactical Themes

As White — Themes to Prepare

  • d4 central break timing: Don't rush d4. Build Re1, Nbd2, Nf1-Ng3 first. Play d4 when Inti's pieces are already committed and the ...d5 break costs him more than it gains.
  • h3 prophylaxis: Always before ...Bg4 is possible. Inti plays ...Bg4 as a standard Italian weapon — neutralize it immediately.
  • ...Nf4 alert: Inti repeatedly maneuvers a knight to f4 (Copa R1: ...Nh5-Nf4; Copa R5: ...Nh5-Nf4; Agua R1: ...Nh5-Nf4). Calculate whether ...Nf4 is coming before releasing central tension.

As Black — Themes to Prepare

  • ...b5-b4 queenside break: Inti's Sicilian White play collapses when Black gets active queenside counterplay before f4-e5 arrives. The ...b4 push disrupts the Nc3 anchor.
  • Kingside castle is mandatory: See warn-box above.
  • ...e5 as response to f4: Know when to close the kingside vs when to play ...d5. Against Inti's f4-e5 push, ...e5 seals the f-file and leaves White with nothing.
