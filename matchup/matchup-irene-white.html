System A — French Tarrasch 3.Nd2 (Primary)

A1 — Tarrasch with f4-h4 pawn storm (Austin's weapon)
The Tarrasch (3.Nd2) is Austin's standard French choice. After 3...c5 4.exd5 exd5 (or 4...Qxd5) White builds with Ngf3, Be2/Bd3, 0-0, and then launches the kingside pawn storm. The f4-h4 plan is particularly effective against French Tarrasch because Black's kingside is structurally undeveloped.
1.e4 e6 2.d4 d5 3.Nd2 c5 4.exd5 exd5 5.Ngf3 Nc6 6.Bb5 Bd6 7.dxc5 Bxc5 8.0-0 Nge7 9.Nb3 Bb6 10.Re1 0-0 11.Bg5 f6 12.Bh4 ±
Key idea: After 0-0, Re1, Bg5/Bh4 targeting f6. If Black plays ...g5 to kick the bishop, play Bg3 and continue with Nbd4. The pawn structure gives White clear queenside expansion while Black has a mobile center that can be blockaded.
Tarrasch Chist.: 3...c5 4.exd5 Qxd5 5.Ngf3 cxd4 6.Bc4 Qd6 7.0-0 Nf6 8.Nb3 Nc6 9.Nbxd4 Nxd4 10.Nxd4 a6 = (equal, familiar to Irene)
