§3 — Recommended Weapon: English Attack (B41/B43)

Recommended: Open Sicilian → English Attack. 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 e6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 Nf6 5.Nc3 a6 6.Be3 d6 7.f3 b5 8.g4! — Austin has practiced this setup vs Zachary (B46 86.6%), Pranav (English Attack), and Emerson. Pablo has no documented games against 8.g4! as Black.

Main Line: English Attack vs Kan (B43)

Austin: 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 e6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 Nf6 5.Nc3 a6 6.Be3 d6 7.f3 b5 8.g4! — The English Attack pawn storm. Cuts off Nf6 development (g4 attacks f5 square), prevents …b4 queenside expansion, and launches direct kingside attack. Pablo has never faced this as Black.
VAR A 8…Nfd7 (solid retreat — most common)
8…Nfd7 9.Qd2 Nc6 10.O-O-O b4 11.Nxc6 bxc3 12.bxc3 → White attacks

After 12.bxc3, White has open b-file, mobile center (f3/g4/h4 next), and Black's queenside counterplay is neutralized. Austin plays 13.h4 threatening h5 and g5 to trap Nf6.

VAR B 8…Bb7 (common — queenside bishop develops)
8…Bb7 9.g5 Nfd7 10.h4 Nc6 11.Qd2 Nxd4 12.Bxd4 → dominant

After 9.g5 Nfd7, White's pawn storm is unstoppable. 10.h4 prepares h5. Pablo's queenside play is too slow against h4-h5-g6 or h5-h6. This is the sharpest line and Austin should know it to move 15.

VAR C Pure Kan: 4…a6 (move-order variation)
4.Nxd4 a6 5.Nc3 Qc7 6.Bd3 Nf6 7.O-O d6 8.f4 Nc6 → f4/g4 attack

If Pablo plays 4…a6 (pure Kan) instead of 4…Nf6, enter the B41/B43 English Attack with f4 instead. After 8.f4, plan f5 or g4-g5 attack. The structure is similar and Austin knows the ideas.
