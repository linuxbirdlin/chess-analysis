  • Move: Bg1 (best g4 — active advance) — CPL 355, +0.29→−3.26. Bishop retreated passively to g1 instead of the active g4 push; one move threw away White's slight advantage.
  • Next move: Na4 (best Rhe1 — rook centralization) — CPL 320, −0.27→−3.47. Knight leapt to the rim when rook activation was correct; second consecutive error from near-equal.

Bg1+Na4 defines the exploit pattern. From +0.29 to −3.47 in two moves. Austin should create positions where Justin must choose between active/passive piece moves around moves 20–30. Justin's tendency to retreat (Bg1 to g1, Na4 to rim) will recur.
