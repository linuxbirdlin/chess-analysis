R8 (PAM 2026) and R7 (Canada Closed) both feature 3+ king-endgame blunders at CPL 1000. In both cases Aarush played the king to losing squares in rook-and-pawn endgames. This is a confirmed, recurring, high-severity weakness.

Time Pressure Correlation

The R6 game (5 blunders, 5 mistakes, 100 CPL) is the clearest time-pressure collapse in the dataset. The error rate accelerates in moves 60–100, strongly suggesting clock stress. Austin should aim to reach complex endgames where both players are short on time — Aarush's accuracy degrades sharply.
