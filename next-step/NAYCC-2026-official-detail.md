North American Youth Chess Championship 2026

by Comité Organizador de la Copa Revolución
 
721
North American Youth Chess Championship 2026 takes place from November 10 to November 17, 2026 at Hotel del Prado in Mexico City, Mexico. The main nine-round Standard championships are played from November 11 to November 16.

The championship is open to youth players representing the three North American members of the Confederation of Chess for Americas: Canada, Mexico and the United States. Players compete in U8, U10, U12, U14, U16 and U18, with separate Open and Girls championships in every age group.

Each Standard section is FIDE-rated and consists of nine Swiss-system rounds at 90 minutes plus 30 seconds per move. The programme also includes separate FIDE Rapid and Blitz tournaments, while leading finishers can earn medals and direct FIDE titles or norms under the applicable regulations.

✅ Eligibility
Players must represent Canada, Mexico or the United States.

Each national federation may register as many players as it wishes. However, players from Canada and the United States require federation approval unless the CCA determines otherwise under the applicable regulations.

Mexican players, as representatives of the host federation, do not require that additional federation endorsement.

Official Players

Each national federation may nominate one Official Player in each of the 12 Standard categories.

Therefore, a federation may nominate up to 12 Official Players.

The organizer covers 60% of accommodation and meal costs for up to 12 Official Players and one Head of Delegation, provided that the delegation includes at least four Official Players.

The deadline to register as an Official Player is:

September 10, 2026 at midnight – Mexico City time

Players registered after this deadline are classified as Additional Players and must pay the corresponding fees.

Minors & Responsible Adults

Every player under 18 must be accompanied by a responsible adult, such as:

Parent
Legal guardian
Coach
Other formally designated adult
The responsible adult must also be registered with the organizer.

One adult may be responsible for several minors.
